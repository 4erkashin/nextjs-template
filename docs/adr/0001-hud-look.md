# HUD look, not neon city

Product UI follows the HUD references: hard cuts, hairlines, invert on hover, press on click. Glitch and color split are short events. Glow is a signal on scan or alert, not the rest or hover of a control.

The usual reading of "cyberpunk UI" is night-city: neon fill, idle glitch, elastic bounce. Those tropes fight the deck screens we treat as source, and they would replace invert as the hover language. Bounce is rejected; press is the mechanical click.
